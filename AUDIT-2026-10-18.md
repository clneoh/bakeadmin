## The last six — found in the same audit, left open for your decision, then FIXED in v549

You said "fix the six open ones". Here they are, each with what it was and what it now does.

### 1. A refund after editing an order down was deducted twice — **fixed**
- **Was:** an order is RM30 and paid. You edit it down to RM15, accept "Refund RM15?" — **the order's takings read RM0**, although you kept RM15.
- **Why:** the edit had already taken the RM15 out of your takings; the refund took it off again.
- **Now:** the refund is still recorded (your receipt register still says the money went back), but it does not move the takings a second time. The order reads **RM15** — the money in your hand. An ordinary refund, on an order you have not edited, is unchanged.

### 2. A discount bigger than the goods broke the printed totals — **fixed**
A RM500 code on RM30 of bread printed lines summing to −RM470 under a Total of RM0.00. ⭐ The discount is now capped at what there is to take off, so every printed surface adds up to its own Total.

### 3. The refund box offered a cash-on-delivery charge you never received — **fixed**
It read "Ticked so far RM24.00" beside "up to RM16.00", then refused the press. ⭐ A COD fee goes to the courier at the door and is no longer offered.

### 4. The Note / tracking popup did not re-price a delivery-type code — **fixed**
Change the delivery charge there and its Total disagreed with the saved order. ⭐ It re-values the code against the charge in the box now.

### 5. Two shop promo faults — **fixed**
A `?promo=` link was refused before the codes arrived and never re-judged; a code's "add RM14 more" stayed on screen after the RM14 was added. ⭐ A code the page has judged is now judged again whenever the basket or the codes change.

### 6. Two shop display faults — **fixed**
The menu kept the last customer's quantity boxes after an order; and "Today's bake has closed" showed on a day the bakery never bakes. ⭐ Both fixed — the heading now asks whether today is one of your bake days.

⚠️ **Two of these six (#4 and #5) could not be given a test that drives the screen to that exact point.** They are verified by reading and by running the same judgement the rest of the shop uses. That is said plainly rather than dressed up.
